01 · Daily Timing

Your three days at a glance

Each day runs from 09:00 to 17:00 (London time, GMT), with 6.5 hours of teaching, morning and afternoon refreshment breaks and a lunch break. On Day 1, please arrive by 08:30 to register and collect your welcome pack. Certificates are presented at the close on Wednesday, so if you're flying home that day, please book an evening flight.

03 · Getting There

From the airport to the venue

Most international flights land at Heathrow (LHR) or Gatwick (LGW). Both have fast rail links into central London, and the hotel is a short walk from Marble Arch station. Choose your airport below.

About 25 km to the hotel 45–55 min by train, 45–75 min by road
Recommended

Elizabeth line

≈ 35 min + short walk
  1. Follow signs for Elizabeth line trains from any terminal (no need to book; tap in with a contactless card).
  2. Ride directly to Bond Street, with no changes.
  3. Walk west along Oxford Street to the hotel (about 10 minutes), or take the Central line one stop west to Marble Arch.

07 · Practical Information

Before you travel

A few things worth knowing for your three days in London.

Visa & ETA

Check whether you need a visa or an Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) to visit the UK at gov.uk/check-uk-visa. If you need a letter confirming your registration, please contact us.

Time zone

In November, London is on Greenwich Mean Time (GMT, UTC+0). All course times on this page are local.

Weather

Mid-November is cool and often damp, typically around 6–12 °C, and it's dark by about 16:30. Bring a warm coat and an umbrella.

Laptop or tablet

Recommended for following the slides. Download the PDF before you travel and bring your charger. See checklist.

Dress code

Business casual throughout the course. Meeting rooms can be cool, so a layer is useful.

Currency

Pound sterling (GBP). Contactless cards and phone payments are accepted almost everywhere, including on public transport.

Power

230 V with UK three-pin sockets (type G). Bring an adaptor if needed.

What's included

Tuition, daily lunch, morning and afternoon refreshments, the course slides in PDF and a certificate of completion, presented at the close on Wednesday.

Questions before you arrive?

Our team is happy to help with travel, accommodation, visa letters or anything else about the course.
