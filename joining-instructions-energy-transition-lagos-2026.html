03 · Arriving Each Day

Getting to the venue

Sessions start promptly at 09:00 and run to a full timetable, so arriving on time each morning helps the whole group. A few tips for a smooth start to each day.

Plan ahead

Allow for traffic

Aim to arrive by 08:45
  • Morning traffic onto Victoria Island is heaviest from about 07:00 to 09:30, especially across the Third Mainland Bridge and along the Lekki–Epe Expressway.
  • Check live traffic before you set off and leave earlier than you would for the office.
  • On Day 1, please be there by 08:30 for registration.

Driving

By car

On-site parking
  • Enter through the main gate on Adetokunbo Ademola Street. Vehicles go through a security check at the gate.
  • Tell the security team you're attending the London Petro Academy training at the hotel.
  • Park in the hotel car park and walk to the main lobby.

04 · Course Materials

Your course materials

All materials are in a shared Google Drive folder: 12 files, about 13 MB in total. Please download the whole folder to your laptop before Day 1 (in Google Drive, choose Download all) so you can work offline during the course.

  • Slides (PowerPoint)
  • Fiscal model (Excel, with macros)
  • Exercise files (Excel)
  • Guides & reference (Word, PDF)
Open course materials

Important · Computer-based course

Please bring your own laptop

This is a hands-on, computer-based course. You'll work on the Excel fiscal model and exercises in every session, so each participant must bring their own laptop. Tablets and phones aren't suitable.

Before Monday 16 November, please check with Shell IT that:

  1. You can open the Google Drive link above. Some company networks and laptops block Google Drive.
  2. You can download the files and save them on your laptop.
  3. The desktop version of Microsoft Excel is installed and you can enable macros. The fiscal model is a macro-enabled workbook (.xlsm) and won't work in Excel Online, Google Sheets or the Excel mobile app.
  4. You can open PowerPoint, Word and PDF files.

Please also bring your charger. If IT can't give you access to the folder, email us before Day 1 and we'll arrange another way to get the files to you.

06 · Practical Information

Good to know

A few things to help you get the most out of the week.

Start time

Registration from 08:30 on Monday. Sessions start at 09:00 sharp every day. All times are Lagos time (WAT).

Laptop

Required every day. Desktop Excel with macros enabled, the course files downloaded, and your charger. See checklist.

ID & security

Bring your Shell staff ID or another photo ID. Vehicles and visitors go through a security check at the hotel gate.

Dress code

Business casual throughout the week. The air-conditioned training room can be cool, so a light layer is useful.

Weather

Mid-November is warm and humid, typically around 25–32 °C, with the odd late-season shower. An umbrella in the car is worth it.

Attendance

Please sign the attendance sheet each morning. Certificates are issued on completion of the full programme.

Phones & email

To keep sessions focused, please keep phones on silent and save calls and email for the breaks.

What's included

Tuition, daily lunch, morning and afternoon refreshments, digital course materials and a certificate of completion, presented at the close on Friday (around lunchtime).

Questions before Day 1?

Our team is happy to help with access to the course materials, laptop set-up or anything else about the programme. You can also contact your Shell Nigeria Learning & Development coordinator.
