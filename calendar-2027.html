About the 2027 Training Calendar

This calendar brings together London Petro Academy's full public course schedule for 2027, spanning our three portfolios: upstream petroleum, downstream oil & gas, and renewable energy. Programmes cover petroleum economics and fiscal systems, gas and LNG contracts, accounting and IFRS for E&P, drilling operations, crude oil markets and trading, power project finance, and the economics of solar, hydrogen and battery storage.

Sessions run from January to December across five world regions — the Americas, Europe, the Middle East, Asia and Africa — in energy hubs including London, Rio de Janeiro, Singapore, Dubai, Doha, Bali, Houston, Aberdeen, Cairo, Lagos, Cape Town, Kuala Lumpur and Santiago. Every course runs in four different regions during the year, while our Brazil-focused programmes are delivered exclusively in Rio de Janeiro and the Indonesia fiscal & JV programme exclusively in Bali. Most classroom courses run as intensive two-to-five-day programmes, with fees starting from £1,600, and every delegate receives a certificate of completion.

Our courses are designed for professionals from national and international oil companies, energy ministries, regulators, utilities, and the banks, law firms and advisory practices that serve the sector. Delegates typically include commercial, finance, legal, planning and technical staff seeking a structured, practitioner-led deep dive into their subject.

To book, use the month tabs above to find a session, then follow through to the course page to reserve your place — our team confirms every booking within one working day. If the dates or locations shown don't fit, any course in the calendar can also be delivered in-house at your offices for groups of six or more delegates.
