Summary: We are London Petro Academy Limited, a specialist energy training provider based in London. We collect your data only to process course bookings, respond to enquiries, and send relevant training information where you've asked us to. We do not sell your data to third parties.

1. Who We Are

London Petro Academy Limited (company number 09875694, VAT 267 3190 90) is a specialist energy training provider registered in England and Wales. Our registered office is at 3rd Floor, 207 Regent Street, London W1B 3HH, United Kingdom.

London Petro Academy is the data controller for the personal data we collect through this website and our training services. This means we are responsible for deciding how and why your personal data is used.

For any data protection matters, please contact us at: info@londonpetroacademy.co.uk

3. How We Collect Your Data

We collect data through:

  • Website forms — course booking forms, enquiry forms, brochure download requests and calendar download forms on londonpetroacademy.co.uk
  • Direct correspondence — emails, phone calls or written communications you send to us
  • Third-party sources — publicly available professional information (e.g. LinkedIn profiles) when we contact you about relevant training programmes
  • Cookies and analytics — automatically when you visit our website (see §8)

6. Who We Share Your Data With

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with:

  • Course instructors and facilitators — limited delegate information (name, organisation, job title) is shared with the course trainer before each session to assist with course preparation.
  • Venue partners — hotel and conference venues receive delegate names to facilitate course logistics (room lists, catering) where applicable.
  • Email and CRM service providers — we use third-party platforms to send booking confirmations and course communications. These providers process data on our behalf as data processors under GDPR-compliant agreements.
  • Accounting and invoicing software — financial data is processed by our accountancy tools to generate invoices and comply with UK financial regulations.
  • Google Analytics — anonymised website usage data is shared with Google for analytics purposes (see §8).
  • Legal or regulatory authorities — we may disclose data where required by law, court order, or regulatory obligation.

All third-party data processors are required to handle your data securely and in accordance with our instructions and applicable data protection law.

7. International Transfers

London Petro Academy delivers training globally, and some of our service providers may process data outside the UK or European Economic Area (EEA). Where this occurs, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as:

  • Reliance on the UK Government's adequacy decisions for certain countries
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the UK ICO
  • Providers that are certified under recognised data protection frameworks

You may request further information about international transfer safeguards by contacting us at info@londonpetroacademy.co.uk.

8. Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses cookies — small text files placed on your device — to help the site function and to understand how it is used. We use:

  • Essential cookies — strictly necessary for the website to operate (e.g. session management, form security). These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics 4) — we use GA4 to collect anonymised information about how visitors use our site (pages viewed, time on site, referral source). IP addresses are anonymised. This helps us improve our website and content.

You may opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

We do not use advertising cookies, social media tracking pixels, or any form of behavioural profiling cookies on this website.

9. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

  • Right of access — to obtain a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — to ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure — to ask us to delete your data where there is no legitimate reason for us to continue processing it.
  • Right to restrict processing — to ask us to pause the processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability — to receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format where processing is based on consent or contract.
  • Right to object — to object to processing based on legitimate interests, including direct marketing.
  • Rights relating to automated decision-making — we do not use automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

To exercise any of these rights, please email info@londonpetroacademy.co.uk with the subject line "Data Subject Request". We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

10. Children's Privacy

Our training programmes and website are intended for professional adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under the age of 18. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a minor, please contact us immediately and we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date at the top. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

If we make significant changes that materially affect how we use your data, we will notify you by email where we hold your contact details.
