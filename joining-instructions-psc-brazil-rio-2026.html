01 · Daily Timing

Your four days at a glance

Each day runs from 09:00 to 17:00 (Rio de Janeiro time, BRT), with morning and afternoon refreshment breaks and a lunch break. Days 1 and 2 cover the legal aspects of PSCs; Days 3 and 4 cover the fiscal and economic aspects. On Day 1, please arrive by 08:30 to register and collect your welcome pack.

02 · Your Trainers

Two specialists, two perspectives

The course is split into two blocks, each led by a specialist in that field. Together they cover how Brazilian PSCs are built in law and how they work in numbers.

TV
Days 1–2 · Mon 23 – Tue 24 Nov

Tom Valentine

Legal aspects of PSCs

Brazil's legal and regulatory framework for production sharing, the institutions behind it, and how the Brazilian model compares with PSC regimes around the world.

  • Petroleum Law, Pre-Salt Law and Law 15,075/2024
  • CNPE, ANP, PPSA and Petrobras
  • International benchmarking and legal risk
A
Days 3–4 · Wed 25 – Thu 26 Nov

Alexey

Fiscal & economic aspects of PSCs

How PSC fiscal terms are designed and how they shape project value for government and investors, ending with a detailed look at Brazil's pre-salt PSCs.

  • Cost recovery, profit oil and royalties
  • Petroleum economics and government take
  • Joint ventures and the pre-salt case study

04 · Arriving Each Day

Getting to the hotel

Sessions start promptly at 09:00 and follow a full timetable, so arriving on time each morning helps the whole group. A few tips for a smooth start to each day.

Plan ahead

Allow for traffic

Aim to arrive by 08:45
  • Traffic into Copacabana is heaviest from about 07:00 to 09:30, especially through the Túnel Novo and Túnel Velho and along Avenida Atlântica.
  • Check live traffic before you set off and leave earlier than you would for the office.
  • On Day 1, please be there by 08:30 for registration.
Live route on Google Maps
Metro

By metro

Cardeal Arcoverde · Line 1
  • Take Line 1 to Cardeal Arcoverde, the closest station to the Leme end of Copacabana.
  • Walk towards the beach and Avenida Atlântica; the hotel is about 10–15 minutes on foot.
  • Pay at the gates with a contactless bank card or your usual transport card.

Recommended · Before Day 1

Bring a laptop or tablet

The slides are shared digitally, so we recommend bringing a laptop or tablet to follow along and keep notes.

Before Day 1, please check that:

  1. You can open the Google Drive link. Some company networks and laptops block Google Drive; if yours does, ask your IT team or download the files from a personal device.
  2. You've downloaded all five PDFs and saved them on your device. They come to about 158 MB, so download them on a good connection rather than relying on the hotel Wi-Fi. Tip: Drive's Download option on the folder saves everything as one zip file.
  3. Your device is charged and you've brought your charger.

If you can't open or download the files, email us before the course and we'll help.

07 · Practical Information

Good to know

A few things worth knowing for your four days at the Hilton Copacabana.

Registration

On Monday 23 November, please arrive by 08:30 to register and collect your welcome pack. Bring photo ID. Dress code is business casual, and the air-conditioned rooms can be cool.

Your certificate

After the course, we'll email you a digital certificate of completion. Its QR code lets anyone scan it to confirm you attended, or check it at londonpetroacademy.co.uk/verify.

Laptop or tablet

Recommended for following the slides. Download the slides before Day 1 and bring your charger. See checklist.

What's included

Tuition, daily lunch, morning and afternoon refreshments, the course slides in PDF and a digital certificate of completion, emailed to you after the course.

Questions before you arrive?

Our team is happy to help with directions, parking, the course materials or anything else about the course.
