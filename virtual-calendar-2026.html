About Live Virtual Training

This calendar lists London Petro Academy's live online training sessions for 2026, currently focused on our renewable energy and petroleum taxation programmes. Courses cover renewable energy economics and finance, solar power finance, corporate and bankable power purchase agreements, hydrogen technology and business models, IPP project finance in emerging markets, and renewable energy auctions.

Virtual courses are delivered live by the same expert practitioners who lead our classroom programmes — these are not pre-recorded modules. Sessions are interactive, built around worked examples, case studies and discussion, and delegates receive full course materials and a certificate of completion.

Start times are scheduled with global audiences in mind, so delegates across Europe, the Middle East, Africa, Asia and the Americas can join at a workable local hour. Formats range from two to five days of half-day or full-day sessions, with fees starting from £1,200 for two-day programmes.

To book, browse the month tabs above and follow through to the course page to reserve your place — our team confirms every booking within one working day. If you have several delegates with the same development need, any virtual course can also be delivered privately for your team, scheduled around your time zone. Classroom editions of many of these courses appear in our main 2026 training calendar.
